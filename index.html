Why you'll love it

No overseas flight, no jet lag, and the biggest scenery on the continent. This spring, trade the ordinary for a wall of blue ice at dawn, humpbacks feeding in Auke Bay, and a Gold Rush town at the end of the rails, all while unpacking just once.

Glaciers, up close

Celebrity Edge sails 30 miles up Endicott Arm to the face of Dawes Glacier, over 600 feet tall and half a mile wide, where calving ice booms with a sound locals call "white thunder."

A ship built for the view

Celebrity Edge, first of the Edge Series: the cantilevered Magic Carpet, the glass-walled Eden, and Infinite Veranda staterooms that open to the sea at the touch of a button.

Travel with your group

53 staterooms are held for Holy Cross, with a confirmed late-seating dinner at 8:30 PM so the whole group sits down together every night.

Easy to get to

Sail round-trip from Seattle's Pier 91: a simple domestic flight, no overseas connections, and a closed-loop cruise that begins and ends at the same U.S. port.

The ports

Four ports, a glacier fjord & the Inside Passage

From the Salmon Capital of the World to a Gold Rush boomtown and a Victorian harbour, every stop on the Dawes Glacier route.

❄ Day 3 · Sun, May 30 · 6:00 AM – 4:00 PM

KetchikanSalmon Capital of the World

Creek Street's wooden houses on stilts over the creek, the world's largest collection of standing totem poles, and Misty Fjords' 3,000-foot granite cliffs by boat or floatplane.
❄ Day 4 · Mon, May 31 · 5:30 – 10:00 AM

Endicott Arm & Dawes GlacierScenic cruising

A 30-mile fjord in the 653,000-acre Tracy Arm-Fords Terror Wilderness, ending at a glacier over 600 feet tall and half a mile wide. Listen for the "white thunder" of calving ice.
❄ Day 4 · Mon, May 31 · 1:30 – 10:00 PM

JuneauAlaska's capital

The only state capital you can't reach by road. Mendenhall Glacier is about 12 miles from the dock, and Auke Bay's humpbacks feed here from May to September.
❄ Day 5 · Tue, Jun 1 · 6:30 AM – 6:00 PM

SkagwayGateway to the Klondike

Ride the White Pass & Yukon Route, begun in 1898, climbing 2,865 feet to the summit in just 20 miles of narrow-gauge track. The Broadway Dock is a five-minute walk from town.
❄ Day 5 · Tue, Jun 1 · 6:30 – 10:30 PM

Alaska Inside PassageScenic cruising

An evening sail through a sheltered waterway of 1,000 islands, where the Alexander Archipelago holds back the Pacific weather and early June brings roughly 18 hours of daylight.
❄ Day 7 · Thu, Jun 3 · 5:30 – 11:59 PM

Victoria, BCAn evening ashore

The Inner Harbour, the Fairmont Empress and the Parliament Buildings outlined in thousands of lights after sunset. Butchart Gardens' 55 acres are a short excursion away.

Signature moments

Big country, unforgettable details

The small moments that turn a cruise into the story you'll tell for years.

Day 4 · 5:30 AM

Dawes Glacier at dawn

Celebrity Edge enters Endicott Arm at 5:30 AM. Bring coffee to the rail, or your Infinite Veranda, as the fjord narrows toward the ice. Harbor seals often lounge on the floes.

Day 4 · until 10 PM

A long day in Juneau

Eight and a half hours in port: Mendenhall Glacier and Nugget Falls, whale watching in Auke Bay, and a downtown that sits steps from the ship.

Day 5

Skagway's Gold Rush

In 1897–98, ships landed thousands of gold seekers here on their way to the Klondike. Ride the 1898 railway over the White Pass, a trail the stampeders climbed on foot.

Day 5 · evening

Sailing the Inside Passage

From 6:30 to 10:30 PM the ship threads the sheltered channels of the Inside Passage. Find a spot on the Rooftop Garden or the Magic Carpet and watch the northern light linger.

Day 7 · 5:30 PM

Victoria at twilight

An evening call in British Columbia's capital: the Inner Harbour, the castle-like Fairmont Empress, and the Parliament Buildings lit by thousands of lights after sunset.

Every evening · 8:30 PM

Dinner together at 8:30

Late seating is confirmed for the Holy Cross group, so the table is yours each night. Afterward, live entertainment in the Grand Plaza or a nightcap in Eden's three glass-walled levels.
